# Admissible-Existence

**Admissible-Existence develops the formal and executable foundations for deciding when states and transitions remain coherent, recoverable, and admissible.**

The organization brings together related formalisms and validation work around existence, transition geometry, consequence, continuity, standing, recoverability, and composition. Its repositories include the AE/RTG/GTG/TT/STCM family, supporting validation factories, and adjacent research surfaces.

## What belongs here

Work in this organization is intended to answer questions such as:

- What makes an entity or state admissibly existent rather than merely present?
- Which transitions preserve coherence and recoverability?
- How should local validity compose across coupled systems?
- What evidence is required to support an admissibility claim?
- How can formal definitions be tested, falsified, and reconstructed?

## Relationship to StegVerse

Admissible-Existence supplies formal structure used by StegVerse governance and execution systems. The organization does **not** turn mathematical research, validation output, or repository presence into execution authority. Runtime authority remains with the applicable StegVerse execution and credential boundaries.

## Repository families

Representative areas include:

- **AE / Existence** — admissible existence and successor-state semantics
- **RTG / GTG / TT / STCM** — transition geometry, transition structure, and state-transition formalisms
- **IW / HPS / FI / related research** — additional formal dimensions and hypotheses
- **validation factories / validators** — independent executable tests and counterexample work
- **public formalism surfaces** — selected documentation and educational material

This is an active research organization. Individual repositories state their own maturity, proof status, and authority boundaries.
