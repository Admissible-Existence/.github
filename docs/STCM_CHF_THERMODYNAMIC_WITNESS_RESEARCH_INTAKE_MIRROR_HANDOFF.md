## Candidate minimal common specimen contract
An immutable, bounded specimen consists of:
1. `schema_version`, `specimen_id`, exact byte digest of frozen source payload and profile.
2. Declared initial state and resulting state with exact canonicalized hashes and source-format/version.
3. Transition identifier, requested effect versus independently established actual effect, explicit causal relation (with lineage/predecessor proof), and observed time basis (not causal proof by itself).
4. Independent witness provenance, identity, source digest, measurement/derivation classification, uncertainty, units, scope and custody proof.
5. Unknown/not_observed/not_applicable preserved explicitly; absent independent evidence is not an affirmative fact.

Independent source-native outputs:
- STCM: finite activation/capacity, phase parameter, conservation-record closure, receipt/causal relation and unresolved accounting.
- CHF: consequence horizon crossed or not, irreversibility claim and evidence, absorption capacity, observability, recoverability and coherence.
- Kevin's interface (only after an authorized specimen export): exact irreversible erasure count `n`, temperature and units, calculated lower bound `Q_min=n*k_B*T*ln(2)`, physical measurement reference **if measured**, measurement uncertainty, native OPEN/HALT gate and exact external receipt digest.
- Comparison output: hashes of both native outputs bound to the same specimen, correspondence/residual matrix, competing and unobserved interpretations, no inherited authority.
