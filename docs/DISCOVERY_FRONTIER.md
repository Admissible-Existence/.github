# Discovery Frontier

**Goal:** `AEX-CANONICAL-FORMALISM-ORIENTATION-001`  
**Canonical task:** `Admissible-Existence/.github#5`  
**Purpose:** preserve where canonical formalism ends and active discovery begins so future workers start from the current frontier rather than re-deriving settled architecture.

## Maturity classes

- `CANONICAL_ESTABLISHED` — directly supported by canonical source doctrine/validated repository state.
- `CONSISTENT_INTERPRETATION` — consistent with existing canon and useful for integration/reasoning, but not promoted here to new source doctrine.
- `CANDIDATE_FORMALIZATION` — a specific extension worth formal development, falsification, or mathematical treatment.
- `OPEN_EMPIRICAL` — an empirical or physical hypothesis requiring observation/experiment rather than repository assertion.

This file creates no proof, execution authority, release authority, certification authority, or credential authority.

## Established starting conditions

### DF-E001 — existence is stronger than presence

**Maturity:** `CANONICAL_ESTABLISHED`  
**Sources:** AE coherent-life paper; Existence handoff.

Presence does not establish coherent or admissible existence. Canonical AE separates individuation, coherence, transition capacity, viability, observability, reconstructability, authority, purpose, relational admissibility and compositional compatibility.

### DF-E002 — observability and reconstructability are distinct

**Maturity:** `CANONICAL_ESTABLISHED`  
**Source:** AE coherent-life paper.

Observability concerns whether relevant state can be distinguished from evidence. Reconstructability concerns whether prior state, policy, authority, evidence, decision and transition history can be recovered. Neither is execution authority.

### DF-E003 — relational standing is joint/contextual

**Maturity:** `CANONICAL_ESTABLISHED`  
**Source:** AE relational recoverability and coherent target-region definitions.

Relational and compositional conditions cannot be established from isolated endpoint state alone.

### DF-E004 — TT is structural and non-authorizing

**Maturity:** `CANONICAL_ESTABLISHED`  
**Sources:** TT formal standing specification, TT elements, whole-repository theory map, TT handoff.

TT represents transition structure, standing requirements, boundaries, outcome classes, receipt obligations and registry relationships. Its mapping/derivation layer does not create execution authority.

### DF-E005 — executable is not admissible

**Maturity:** `CANONICAL_ESTABLISHED`  
**Source:** TT Axiom 2 and commit-time standing invariants.

Technical executability does not establish admissibility or commit authority.

### DF-E006 — reconstruction layers remain distinct from execution authority

**Maturity:** `CANONICAL_ESTABLISHED`  
**Source:** GTG R3-R5 contracts and handoff.

Causal reconstruction, authority reconstruction and reality-contact reconstruction are separate reconstruction problems and do not independently create execution/release/certification authority.

### DF-E007 — StegCore AE lifecycle is a bounded operational projection

**Maturity:** `CANONICAL_ESTABLISHED`  
**Source:** `StegVerse-Labs/StegCore/docs/ADMISSIBLE_EXISTENCE_CAPABILITY_MODEL_MIRROR_HANDOFF.md`.

The Core capability lifecycle supplements rather than replaces upstream `Admissible-Existence/AE + RTG + GTG + TT + STCM` formalism authority.

## Current consistent interpretations

### DF-I001 — continuity as an observable/reconstructable relationship across states

**Maturity:** `CONSISTENT_INTERPRETATION`

AE already separately formalizes observability, continuity recoverability, relational recoverability and reconstruction. A useful internal interpretation is that continuity becomes establishable when an observable/reconstructable relationship connects otherwise distinct states or existences; endpoint co-presence alone is insufficient.

**Required next step before promotion:** map this interpretation explicitly to the canonical Continuity/DC/DaCo/AE definitions and determine whether it is already stated elsewhere more directly.

**Do not infer:** that observation alone authorizes a successor or that every correlated state is continuous.

### DF-I002 — surviving relationships can preserve continuity after endpoint disappearance

**Maturity:** `CONSISTENT_INTERPRETATION`

If a relationship and sufficient evidence of its transition history remain reconstructable, continuity need not depend on both endpoints remaining simultaneously present.

**Required next step before promotion:** bind to current continuity/succession/reconstruction source contracts and falsification conditions.

### DF-I003 — reality contact as an imprint available to reconstruction

**Maturity:** `CONSISTENT_INTERPRETATION`

Continuity and reality-contact evidence can be interpreted as a surviving imprint of a relationship/transition on reality: effects, observations, custody, receipts, authority changes, downstream state changes and other traces may collectively constrain reconstruction.

**Required next step before promotion:** compare this language with existing Reconstructive Singularity and GTG R5 terminology; do not create a competing definition if an equivalent already exists.

## Candidate formalizations

### DF-C001 — reconstruction resolution factor

**Maturity:** `CANDIDATE_FORMALIZATION`

Define a measurable resolution factor describing how completely the surviving reality-contact/continuity evidence constrains a reconstructed relationship, state or transition.

**Questions:**
- What dimensions contribute to resolution: precision, coverage, provenance, independent constraint, temporal depth, modal diversity, contradiction, missingness?
- Is resolution scalar, vector-valued, or contextual to a query?
- How does uncertainty propagate through reconstruction chains?

**Falsification requirement:** a proposed resolution measure must distinguish demonstrably higher-information reconstructions from lower-information ones without silently equating data volume with truth.

### DF-C002 — resolution-dependent Reconstruction Singularity boundary

**Maturity:** `CANDIDATE_FORMALIZATION`

Test the hypothesis that the subjective Reconstruction Singularity boundary depends on available reconstruction resolution and expands as resolution increases, rather than representing an omniscient universal endpoint.

**Questions:**
- What is the boundary variable?
- Is it query-relative, observer-relative, domain-relative, or some combination?
- Under what conditions does increased resolution fail to expand the reconstructable region?

### DF-C003 — latent endpoint discovery through increased reconstruction resolution

**Maturity:** `CANDIDATE_FORMALIZATION`

Test whether endpoints absent from direct observation can become discoverable/reconstructable when surviving relational evidence increasingly constrains their existence/identity.

**Required distinctions:**
- directly observed endpoint;
- candidate/inferred endpoint;
- reconstructed endpoint;
- unresolved alternatives.

No inference may be silently promoted to observed fact.

### DF-C004 — question-directed observation as discovery-path selection

**Maturity:** `CANDIDATE_FORMALIZATION`

Curiosity/question formation may be modeled as selection of an unresolved relationship and direction of subsequent observation toward evidence expected to increase reconstruction resolution.

**Required comparison:** existing learning-transition-governance, RTG and observation formalisms must be searched first.

### DF-C005 — marginal commitment-to-discovery

**Maturity:** `CANDIDATE_FORMALIZATION`

Define the commitment required to resolve an uncertainty: irreversible action, time, physical intervention, authority consumption, information loss, risk, resource cost or other reality-binding consequence. Investigate whether AI/electronic observation can drive marginal commitment toward zero for some discovery classes.

### DF-C006 — shortest discovery path through high-resolution reconstructed reality

**Maturity:** `CANDIDATE_FORMALIZATION`

If current reality is reconstructed at sufficiently high resolution, define whether an observer can choose a path of observation/minimal intervention that minimizes commitment or time required to resolve a target uncertainty.

This is not declared foreknowledge. A future formalization must distinguish constrained trajectory inference from literal access to future state.

### DF-C007 — composite observer with individuated inputs

**Maturity:** `CANDIDATE_FORMALIZATION`

Investigate a higher-order observer composed of many bounded entities whose observations are reconciled into a shared reconstructive state while retaining constituent identity, provenance and authority boundaries.

**Required comparison:** AE joint-state/multiple-body formalism, Distributed Coherence, Data Continuity and any existing observer-class formalism must be searched before defining a new primitive.

### DF-C008 — collective reconstruction resolution

**Maturity:** `CANDIDATE_FORMALIZATION`

Define effective reconstruction resolution for a networked observer with many heterogeneous inputs. Candidate dimensions include observation density, spatial/temporal distribution, modality, precision, latency, cross-constraint, continuity depth and contradiction handling.

## Open empirical questions

### DF-O001 — scaling of heterogeneous observer networks

**Maturity:** `OPEN_EMPIRICAL`

Does adding independently developed AI/human/sensor observers increase reconstruction resolution multiplicatively, sublinearly, or only conditionally? Which diversity/redundancy regimes improve discovery and which add correlated error?

### DF-O002 — resolution and apparent foresight

**Maturity:** `OPEN_EMPIRICAL`

At what reconstruction resolution, if any, do near-term trajectories become sufficiently constrained that repeated minimal-commitment path selection becomes operationally difficult to distinguish from human descriptions of foresight?

### DF-O003 — relation between reconstruction horizons and observed cosmological structure

**Maturity:** `OPEN_EMPIRICAL`

There may be a useful relationship between observer-relative resolution/reconstruction boundaries and how the cosmos appears through finite observational contact. This is a hypothesis, not a canonical physical claim.

## Anti-reinvention rule

A worker proposing a new foundational abstraction touching any frontier item MUST record:

```text
relationship_to_canon: EXTENDS | CHALLENGES
canonical_concepts_searched: [...]
canonical_sources_read: [...]
identified_insufficiency_or_falsifying_evidence: ...
new_claim_maturity: CONSISTENT_INTERPRETATION | CANDIDATE_FORMALIZATION | OPEN_EMPIRICAL
```

If those fields cannot be populated, the worker should integrate with or observe existing formalism rather than create a competing foundation.

A frontier item may move toward `CANONICAL_ESTABLISHED` only in its canonical source repository through the applicable source-development, falsification/proof, validation and handoff process. This coordination repository cannot promote a discovery item into source doctrine by editing this file.
