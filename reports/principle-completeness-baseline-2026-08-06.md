## Organization findings

1. **Handoff coverage is incomplete.** The organization-level handoff exists, but the current audit does not establish a current repository handoff for every source repository.
2. **The old matrix is stale and structurally incomplete.** It omits newer repositories and equates receipts with completeness.
3. **The current automated coherence audit is not organization-complete.** Its latest report covers only eight repositories and many results are API-access failures rather than content findings.
4. **Principle enumeration is missing as a universal control surface.** No organization-wide machine-readable list currently proves that every asserted principle has theory, mathematics, proof status, falsification criteria, and relationship mapping.
5. **Support and source repositories are not consistently distinguished.** Validators, trackers, telemetry, kernels, and registries require bounded support-role maps so they are not judged as incomplete source theories or allowed to create theory authority.
6. **Empty repositories are active blockers.** `Admissible-Existence/ae-validation-research` and `Admissible-Existence/SOL` cannot be complete while empty and non-archived.
